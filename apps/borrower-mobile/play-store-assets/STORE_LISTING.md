## Full description

Phindu Credit Solutions helps borrowers in Zambia apply for personal and salary-backed loans, track applications, upload KYC documents, sign contracts, and manage repayments — securely from one app.

**What you can do**
- Sign in with your mobile number (OTP)
- Apply for eligible loan products
- Upload NRC, payslips, and supporting documents
- View loan status and repayment schedule
- Submit payment information and download receipts

**Security & trust**
- Official app for Phindu Credit Solutions Limited
- Your data is handled in line with our Privacy Policy
